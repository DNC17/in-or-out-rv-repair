01 Who fixes it

Adam and his crew have been keeping Manitoba RVs on the road since long before it was a shop on Wenzel Street.

Not a dealership

No service advisor, no three‑week queue, no runaround. You talk to the person holding the multimeter. Text a photo of the problem and you'll often get an answer before anybody charges you a service call.

Parts on the shelf

Faucets, tongue jacks, toilets, vents, furnaces, water heaters. We stock the things that actually strand people, so a Friday breakdown doesn't become a write‑off weekend.

Prairie‑proof work

Winnipeg winters are brutal on a trailer. Winterizing, thawed lines, cracked tanks, soft roofs and rotted framing — we've seen every way the cold gets in, and how to keep it out.

04 How it goes

Four steps. No mystery.

01

Call or text a photo

Describe it, or send a picture of the part, the panel or the puddle. Half of it gets sorted right there — and that's a service call you didn't pay for.

02

Honest diagnosis

You get told what's actually wrong, what it'll take, and what it'll cost — including when the right answer is "that's not worth fixing."

03

In or out

Small and urgent? We come to you. Big, structural or cold outside? It comes into the heated bay on Wenzel Street.

04

Back on the road

Fixed, tested and explained — so you know what happened and how to spot it coming next time.
